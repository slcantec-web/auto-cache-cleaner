Why Auto Cache Cleaner

Built for people who want control, not another always-on service.

On-demand only

Runs only when you ask it to. Zero idle RAM or battery consumption in the background.

See before you clear

Scan to see how much cache every app is using, then jump straight to the ones worth clearing.

Custom reminders

Choose a reminder every 3, 7, 14 or 30 days — or turn it off completely.

Privacy first

No accounts, no analytics. The app only fetches a tiny public file to check for updates.

How it works

Four simple steps. No learning curve.

  1. Open the app when you need extra storage space.
  2. Tap Clear Cache to reclaim space, or scan apps first to see what’s using the most.
  3. Pick a reminder schedule Default is every 7 days. Change it anytime.
  4. Close the app It won’t bother you until your next reminder.

Installing

Download the APK, open it, and allow installs from your browser or file manager when Android asks. The app tells you when a new version is out, and has a Check for update button on its main screen.
