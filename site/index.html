Why Auto Cache Cleaner

On-demand only

Runs only when you ask it to. No idle RAM or battery consumption.

See it before you clear it

Scan to see how much cache every app is using, then jump straight to the ones worth clearing.

Custom reminders

Choose a reminder every 3, 7, 14 or 30 days, or turn it off.

Privacy first

No accounts, no analytics. The app only fetches a small public file to check for updates.

How it works

  1. Open the app when you need extra storage.
  2. Tap Clear Cache to reclaim your space.
  3. Pick a reminder schedule. The default is every 7 days.
  4. Close the app. It won't bother you until your next reminder.

Installing

Download the APK, open it, and allow installs from your browser or file manager when Android asks. The app tells you when a new version is out, and has a Check for update button on its main screen.
